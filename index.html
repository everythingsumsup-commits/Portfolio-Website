INDEPENDENT RESEARCH In progress ↗

Infinity Vision: A New Frame for Premium Cinema?

I've been exploring Disney's Infinity Vision initiative and its place in the premium cinema market. What caught my attention was how it compares with established formats like IMAX, and what it could mean for studios, cinema chains, and audiences. It's still an early-stage project, but one I'm keen to explore further.

I worked with a five-member team for the IIMB CDPG 2026 case challenge. We explored DigiYatra's potential expansion into commercial applications.
